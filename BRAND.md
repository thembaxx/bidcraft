# Bidcraft

**Make every bid count.**

The name joins the discipline of bidding with the care of craft. Use **Bidcraft** in prose and the lowercase **bidcraft.** wordmark in the interface. Voice: clear, considered, useful. Prefer evidence-backed claims and practical guidance over promises of guaranteed compliance or awards.

The supplied image of dimensional app icons directly informed the rounded dark tile, tactile folded surfaces, precise highlights, and sculptural B. The mark combines two document ribbons in ivory and chartreuse, with teal inner surfaces. The SVG favicon is a simplified companion mark for small sizes.

| Token      | Color     | Use                            |
| ---------- | --------- | ------------------------------ |
| Ink        | `#101714` | Logo field, primary typography |
| Paper      | `#F8F6EF` | Canvas                         |
| Teal       | `#168B7A` | Emphasis and evidence          |
| Chartreuse | `#DDFB82` | Primary actions and logo       |

Typography: Urbanist for the interface, JetBrains Mono for references and scores, a restrained italic serif accent for the headline. Keep generous whitespace around the logo. Use it on a quiet background and preserve its proportions.

Assets in `public/brand`: original generated PNG, optimized WebP, 32/180/192/512-pixel PNG icons, and a 1200×630 social card. `public/icon.svg` is the small-size companion mark.

Motion is brief and functional: spring tab indicator, soft view transitions, accessible feedback, modal entry, and gentle 3D movement. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`; never make a task depend on animation. The scene's pause control stops its continuous movement.

Primary audience: South African bid teams. Use South African English, rand (ZAR), SAST, and local procurement terminology. Keep fictional demonstrations clearly distinct from source-backed opportunities.
